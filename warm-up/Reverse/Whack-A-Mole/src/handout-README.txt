WHACK A MOLE
============

Ten by ten. One hundred holes. Somewhere down there is a mole, and it does
not sit still.

You get twelve swings. Choose carefully.

    $ ./mallet

Coordinates are 'C7' (column A-J, row 0-9) or '7,2' (row,col).

Files:
  mallet          the minigame (stripped, x86-64 ELF)
  holes/          hole_00.bin .. hole_99.bin -- read-only, do not edit

Flag format: HIBCHB26{...}
